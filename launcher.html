Shrike Launcher

A third party launcher for Minecraft: Java Edition on Windows. It signs you into your own Microsoft account, downloads the official game files from Mojang, and launches them.

Started November 2026. There is no release and no download link on this page yet.

What it will do

Sign in

Microsoft account sign in, like any other launcher you've used. Your account is completely safe. There is no offline or unauthenticated way to play.

Install the game

Download Minecraft from Mojang's own manifests, check every file, and keep it in its own folder. Nothing of Mojang's is redistributed by us.

Profiles

A Shrike profile with the client and its mods set up, or just play with a normal profile with vanilla, fabric, or neoforge.

Skins and capes

Change your Minecraft skin, switch between the capes your account already owns, and preview both. You can also make custom capes here too which other Shrike users will be able to see.

Where it's up to

Early stages of development currently. The client mod is the finished part of this project and the launcher is just starting. Minecraft 26.2 only to begin with.

Updates will be given at a later date.

Until then

Shrike works as a normal Fabric mod. Drop it in your mods folder with any launcher you already use.
